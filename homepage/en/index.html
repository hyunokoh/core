Frequently asked

Got a question?

If you don't find your answer here, our support team is online 24/7.

What does "balance verification" mean?
Every five minutes, zkCEX publishes a cryptographic snapshot that totals all customer balances. Tap "Verify Balance" in the app and you'll see a proof that your funds are included in the exchange's reserves. It's a safeguard backed by math, not promises.
What are the fees?
Spot trading is 0.05% maker and 0.10% taker — among the lowest in the industry. Deposits are free; withdrawal fees vary by asset and reflect on-chain network costs. High-volume traders automatically receive additional discounts.
What assets can I trade?
BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT and more than 100 other major digital assets across USDT and KRW markets. New listings go through a strict security, liquidity and compliance review. We email you when new markets open.
How is my account secured?
The vast majority of customer assets are held in offline cold storage and require multi-signature approval to withdraw. Accounts get two-factor authentication, withdrawal address allowlists, and real-time anomaly detection. And on top of all that — we prove our reserves every five minutes.
How long do withdrawals take?
For verified accounts, standard withdrawals are processed in about 10 minutes on average. Your first withdrawal or any large amount may go through additional security review; you can track the status live in the app.
