How to read this

  • p95 matters most — it's the latency your slowest 5% of users see.
  • Error rate > 1% on read endpoints almost always means a downstream service is saturated or down.
  • Bottleneck: the container with the highest CPU during the run is the first place to optimize; everything else is usually downstream of it.
  • Re-run with build_report.py after a change to see the Δ.
